What is verified here, and what is not

Programme names, colleges and formats are real. The domain weights, station counts and window months in this prototype are illustrative and are not the programmes' published scoring documents.

Where a college has not published a weighting for the current cycle, the real product marks it as not published. It never carries last year's number forward, because a weighting that moved by five points is exactly the thing that costs someone a post.

Sample questions are written by us from the published domains. They are not leaked questions, and anyone selling you those is selling you something else.
