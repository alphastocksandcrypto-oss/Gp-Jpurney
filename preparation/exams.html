What is verified here, and what is not

Exam names, colleges and stage structures are real. Fees, sitting dates and closing dates in this prototype are illustrative and are not the colleges' published values.

No pass rates appear on this page, on purpose. Publishing a pass rate for a named college's exam without verifying it against that college's own reporting would be exactly the kind of confident guess this product exists to avoid.

Always book through the college. MedNav does not take exam applications or fees, and is not affiliated with any of the colleges listed.
