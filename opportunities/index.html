The same rules that govern every date on MedNav apply to this board.

  • Rechecked regularly. Closed listings come down. Anything we cannot confirm is marked, not quietly left up.
  • Spot an error? Tell us and we review it the same day, then correct or remove the listing.

Before you click apply

What doctors ask about this board.

Do I apply through MedNav?

No. Every listing is aggregated and linked, not hosted. You apply through the original posting on the HSE site or the agency site, and MedNav never receives your application or your CV.

Why do some locum listings show no rate?

Because the agency did not publish one. We show a rate only where the source publishes it, and we never estimate. A blank rate is an honest blank, not a missing number we could have guessed.

How often are listings refreshed?

The board is rechecked regularly against each source, and every card carries the date it was last confirmed. Listings that close are removed rather than left up.

Which sources does MedNav aggregate?

HSE and named agencies for jobs and locums, including LocumLink, Match Medics and Global Medics. Conferences and courses come from the colleges and societies that run them, including RCPI, RCSI, ICGP and the Irish Cardiac Society. Every source is named on the card.

Do conferences and courses count towards my portfolio?

CPD points and attendance feed the portfolio score in Career Tools. At MVP the conferences listing is informational, so you record attendance yourself and MedNav scores it against the criteria your target programme publishes.

Can I get told when something relevant appears?

Yes. Save a search with a free account and MedNav emails you when a matching post, shift or course is added, and again before the deadline closes.
