Questions doctors actually ask

What doctors ask before they create an account.

How is MedNav different from a Qbank or a job board?

Job boards get you a post. Qbanks get you through one paper. MedNav checks your whole route against what a programme actually publishes, and tells you the next action, not just the next practice question.

Is MedNav free to use?

Creating an account, seeing your roadmap, and tracking your gaps against real entry criteria is free. Interview preparation question banks are priced per programme, so you only pay for the interview you're actually sitting.

Does MedNav replace official college guidance?

No. MedNav is an independent platform and is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, RCPI, RCSI, ICGP, the College of Psychiatrists, the IMC or the HSE. Always verify criteria against official entry guidance before applying.

How often is the information on MedNav checked?

We check every date and scoring criterion against the official source, and keep checking regularly. If something's out of date, we say so, not guess.

What happens if a college hasn't published this cycle's scoring criteria yet?

We mark the gap honestly rather than estimate. You'll see exactly which domains are confirmed and which are still pending publication for the current cycle.

Which specialties and pathways does MedNav cover?

Every specialty on the Irish training ladder, from BST and GP training through to HST and consultant posts, including routes for IMGs and doctors returning to Ireland from abroad.
