Before you create an account

What doctors ask about the tools.

Do I need an account to use the career tools?

The worked example on this page runs without one. A roadmap tied to your own profile needs a free account, because it is saved to you and kept current as colleges publish new criteria and dates.

Where do the scoring criteria come from?

Published entry criteria from RCPI, RCSI, ICGP, the College of Psychiatrists and HSE NDTP. Where a college has not published the current cycle's criteria yet, we mark the gap rather than estimate it.

What do the career tools cost?

Your roadmap, gap analyser, application readiness, shortlisting score, deadlines and both explorers are free with an account. Interview preparation question banks are priced per programme, so you only pay for the interview you are actually sitting.

Does it work if I qualified outside Ireland?

Yes. Choose Qualified abroad above and the ladder changes: IMC registration and Irish clinical experience come before the training rungs, because that is the route that actually applies.

Can I change my target specialty later?

At any time. Everything recalculates against the new target and nothing you have already recorded is lost, so you can compare two routes before committing to either.
